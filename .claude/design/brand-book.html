AI OS — Brand Book

The design interface. Roles and boundaries, not examples — design freely inside them. Every contrast ratio below is computed in this page from the hex values, never copied.

How to use this. Reference tokens by role (primary, surface, error), never by hex. A component that hardcodes #3b82f6 breaks the moment the brand moves. If a needed role does not exist, propose adding one — do not improvise a value.

Voice and intent

BrandTechnical precision meets approachable intelligence — the system should feel like a capable expert who speaks plainly.
ColourCool blue-purple spectrum signals trust and innovation. Warm accents (amber, green) carry semantic meaning without competing with primary actions.
TypeInter for neutral clarity in data-dense interfaces; JetBrains Mono signals code-awareness without being intimidating.
ShapeMedium radius (8px) balances professional and friendly — not so sharp it feels cold, not so round it feels playful.
Space4px base grid for mathematical consistency; generous whitespace prevents cognitive overload in data-rich views.

Palette

Ratios are computed against white (#ffffff) and against the page background (#0f1419). AA needs 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries. Several accents fail on white — that is a real constraint, stated rather than hidden: use them on the dark surface, or pair them with a darker shade for text on light.
