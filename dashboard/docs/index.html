Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI OS?

AI OS is the agentic operating system documented here: a Virtual Corporate HQ where an Agent Fleet of 73 specialized sub-agents, across 6 AI models, 4 routing tiers, and 11 departments, handles work such as SEO audits, media production, and monetization pipelines.

How many agents does AI OS have, and how are they organized?

73 specialized sub-agents across 6 AI models, 4 routing tiers, and 11 departments. The Agent Fleet page documents each role, its capabilities, and its escalation paths.

What license options are available?

Community is free and open-source with 19 agents and 6 departments, self-hosted. Business is $1,997 one-time and offers all 73 agents, all 11 departments, self-instance theming, and priority email support. Enterprise is $4,997 one-time and adds SSO/SAML, advanced security, and custom agent development.

What does the Hermes agent do?

Hermes is an always-on MCP background worker that provides remote approvals, walkaway mode, cron jobs, mobile bug diagnosis, and self-improving skills.

How do I start using AI OS?

Follow the Getting Started guide: choose a plan, configure your API keys, and explore your Virtual HQ in under five minutes.

What is the Knowledge Graph?

An auto-categorizing knowledge base with semantic connections, radial visualization, and vault storage that serves as long-term memory for every agent.

What is the Knowledge & Records department?

A governed company document library: a catalog over the Memory Vault, extracted uploads, and agent-output artifacts, with owner, sensitivity, reader-scoped access, and retention on every record. Every agent on every tier reads it through a fenced, untrusted-by-default choke-point. See Knowledge & Records.
