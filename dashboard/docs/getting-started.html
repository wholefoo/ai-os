Getting Started

Get up and running with AI OS in under five minutes. Choose a plan, configure your API keys, and start orchestrating your Virtual Corporate HQ.

Step 1 — Choose a Plan

Visit aiosorchestrationlab.com — AI OS offers five plans:

PlanPriceBest For
Free Demo$0Limited hosted preview, 3 agents, 1 SEO audit/mo, Atlas tour guide
CommunityFreeOpen-source, self-hosted. 19 agents, 6 departments, community support
Business License$1,997 one-timeAll 70 agents, all 11 departments, self-instance theming. No recurring fees
Enterprise License$4,997 one-timeEverything in Business + SSO/SAML, advanced security, custom agents
Managed Website$997 setup + $250/moDone-for-you: we build and host your site on the VPS with a custom domain and TLS, plus a scoped workspace dashboard with Web Studio and your own SEO/AEO audits. Get started

Step 2 — Deploy or Log In

For the Free Demo, log in at aiosorchestrationlab.com/app. For self-hosted plans (Community, Business, Enterprise), clone the repo and deploy on your own infrastructure. Business and Enterprise license holders can deploy on their own custom domain.

AI OS uses your own API keys — you control spend and rate limits. Navigate to Settings in the dashboard sidebar and enter your keys:

The Settings dashboard provides a Test button on each key field — click it after saving to verify the connection works.

Step 5 — Run Your First SEO Audit

Navigate to SEO Agency, enter any domain, and click Audit. Five sub-agents run in parallel:

  1. Keyword Analysis
  2. Technical SEO
  3. Competitor Analysis
  4. Content Analysis
  5. Backlink Profile

After the audit completes, you get a composite score, executive summary, quick wins, and a phased action plan. Use the post-audit buttons to generate content briefs, a 12-week calendar, or optimized meta tags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does AI OS cost?

Free Demo is $0 for a limited hosted preview. Community is free and self-hosted. Business License is $1,997 one-time for all 70 agents and all 11 departments. Enterprise License is $4,997 one-time and adds SSO/SAML, advanced security, and custom agents. Managed Website is $997 setup plus $250/mo for a done-for-you hosted site.

Do I need all the API keys to get started?

No. You only need an Anthropic key to start. The other keys unlock additional features but are not required, and you can add them as you need them.

How many AI agents does AI OS include?

The Virtual HQ shows 70 named AI employees across 11 departments, including the Knowledge & Records library. Behind them, AI OS also runs 11 system and orchestration agents that power the platform without a seat on the org chart, bringing the full fleet to 70 specialized agents.

How do I run my first SEO audit?

Navigate to SEO Agency, enter any domain, and click Audit. Five sub-agents run in parallel: Keyword Analysis, Technical SEO, Competitor Analysis, Content Analysis, and Backlink Profile.

Can Business and Enterprise license holders use a custom domain?

Yes. Business and Enterprise license holders can deploy on their own custom domain, unlike the Free Demo which runs at aiosorchestrationlab.com/app.

How do I verify an API key works?

Each key field in Settings has a Test button. Click it after saving to verify the connection works.
