3. What’s Included (Everything in Business, Plus)

The Enterprise License includes all features of the Business License, plus the following enterprise-grade additions:

  • SSO/SAML Integration — Single sign-on support with major identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, OneLogin);
  • Custom Agent Development Assistance — up to 5 custom agents built with you during Year 1 (Section 4);
  • Early Access to New Features — beta and pre-release features available before general release;

  • Advanced Security Configurations — assistance with hardened deployments, network isolation, and security policy;

4. Custom Agent Development

The Enterprise License includes assistance developing up to 5 custom agents during Year 1. A custom agent is defined as:

  • A new agent role with a specific name, personality, and behavioral profile;
  • Specific model routing configuration (e.g., a particular AI model tier);
  • Custom skills and capabilities tailored to your business processes;
  • Department assignment and integration with your organizational structure.

Custom agent development is collaborative: you define requirements and use cases, and the AI OS team handles implementation, testing, and deployment.

Additional custom agents beyond the initial 5 can be developed at a negotiated rate. Contact support@aiosorchestrationlab.com for pricing.

5. SSO/SAML Integration

The Enterprise License includes SSO/SAML integration support, letting your organization’s users authenticate via your existing identity provider instead of separate AI OS credentials.

Compatible identity providers include:

  • Okta
  • Microsoft Azure Active Directory (Azure AD / Entra ID)
  • Google Workspace
  • OneLogin

Other SAML 2.0–compliant providers may also be supported.
