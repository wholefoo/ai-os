Frequently Asked Questions

What is the AI OS Community Edition?

The free, open-source core of the AI OS platform, released under a source-available, open-core license for self-hosted deployment. It provides a fully functional multi-agent orchestration platform for personal, commercial, and educational use.

What’s included in the Community Edition at no cost?

19 agents across 6 departments (Engineering, Content, SEO, Research, Operations, Knowledge & Records), Scout and Pro model tiers, 1 SEO audit per month, a full knowledge graph, Tech Radar, basic notifications, file-based memory, full custom domain support, and community support via GitHub Issues.

Custom branding, Gemini Omni Creative Studio, YouTube Intelligence, custom agent creation, advanced reporting, the browser automation agent, lead generation, product factory, marketing hub, video meetings, Grok Intel, the design system module, agent builder, and SSO/SAML are not included. A Business ($1,997) or Enterprise ($4,997) license unlocks them.

Yes. You are solely responsible for your own API keys for Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, OpenAI, and any other integrated provider, and for all resulting costs and rate limits.

Is attribution required?

Yes. Public deployments must retain “Powered by AI OS” visibly in the footer. Attribution is not required in source code repositories, development environments, or internal testing deployments that are not publicly accessible.

How do I upgrade to Business or Enterprise?

Business is a $1,997 one-time payment and Enterprise is $4,997. Once your license key is validated, commercial features activate immediately on your existing installation — no data migration or reinstallation required.
