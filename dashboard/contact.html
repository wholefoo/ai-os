Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I get a response?

The AI Helpdesk on this page answers most questions instantly from our documentation. If your issue needs a person, it's logged for our team and we follow up by email.

I'm having trouble deploying. Where do I start?

Start with the Deployment Guide. If you're still stuck, describe the error in the AI Helpdesk above — it can walk you through the fix or log it for the team.

Can I upgrade from Community to Business or Enterprise?

Yes. Visit the pricing page and purchase a license. Your existing data and configuration are preserved — commercial features activate immediately when you add your license key.

Can I brand my own AI OS instance?

Yes. Business ($1,997) and Enterprise ($4,997) licenses include self-instance theming, so you can put your own company name, logo, and colors on your private, self-hosted dashboard. Ask the AI Helpdesk above if you need help configuring your instance.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Use the form above and set the subject to “Security.” Please do not open a public GitHub issue for vulnerabilities. Every report is logged and taken seriously.
