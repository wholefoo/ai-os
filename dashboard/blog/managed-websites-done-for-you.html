Managed Websites, Done For You: $997 Setup + $250/mo Hosting & Maintenance

June 2026 9 min read Product

Not everyone wants to run an AI operating system themselves. So we built a done-for-you path: for a $997 one-time setup plus $250/month hosting & maintenance, the operator builds your website inside AI Web Studio, hosts it on our VPS with a custom domain and TLS, and hands you a scoped workspace dashboard where you can edit your site and run your own SEO and AEO audits. Everything else — deployments, certificate renewals, content updates — is managed for you from the operator's CRM.

The gap this fills

AI OS already ships everything you need to build and run a digital presence: 70 specialized AI agents across 11 departments, AI Web Studio for designing and hosting static sites, and a full SEO + AEO agency for search and answer-engine readiness. The Community edition is free, and the Business and Enterprise licenses unlock the full fleet.

But a license assumes you want to be the operator. Plenty of small businesses, solo founders, and busy teams want the output — a fast, well-built, well-optimized site — without learning the platform, provisioning a server, or babysitting TLS certificates. The Managed Website offer is for them.

You bring the brief. We build it, host it, and keep it healthy — and you still get a real dashboard, not a black box.

What you get

  • A site built for you in AI Web Studio — designed and assembled by the operator using the same design and build agents that power the platform, then deployed as a static site on the VPS.
  • Custom domain + TLS — your own domain, wired up with automatic HTTPS certificates and renewals handled by the hosting agents.
  • A scoped workspace dashboard — your own login into a workspace limited to your account. Inside it you get Web Studio access to edit your pages and your own SEO/AEO audits to track how your site performs.
  • Ongoing hosting & maintenance — uptime, deploys, content updates, and security patches managed by the operator. The $250/month covers the hosting and the human-plus-agent maintenance behind it.
  • Operator management from the CRM — your account lives in the operator's CRM, so requests, updates, and changes are tracked and handled in one place.

The Managed Website is a service layered on top, for people who would rather have it run for them.

Agent-buyable via agentic commerce

The Managed Website offer is designed to be agent-buyable. Through AI OS's agentic-commerce support, an AI agent acting on your behalf can discover the offer, confirm the terms, and complete the purchase — the $997 setup and the recurring $250/month — without a human having to click through a checkout flow manually.

This matters because the same agents that buy the service can also describe the brief. You can hand an agent your requirements and let it transact the setup, and the operator's side picks it up from there. Outward-facing and irreversible steps stay behind Auto-Mode approval gating, so a purchase or a domain change is confirmed before it executes — agentic, but human-in-the-loop where it counts.

What the operator does behind the scenes

On the operator's side, your project runs through the platform's normal machinery:

  1. Build — design and web-build agents assemble the site in AI Web Studio from your brief, with import/export so existing assets can come along.
  2. Host — hosting agents deploy the static site to the VPS, attach your custom domain, and provision TLS.
  3. Optimize — the SEO + AEO agency runs audits so the site is ready for both traditional search and AI answer engines.
  4. Manage — your account is tracked in the CRM, where the operator handles requests, schedules updates, and monitors health.

You see the results of all of this in your scoped dashboard, and you can jump into Web Studio yourself any time you want to make a change.

Who it's for

  • Small businesses that need a credible, fast, optimized site without hiring an agency or a developer.
  • Solo founders who want to ship a landing page or marketing site and move on.
  • Busy teams that would rather pay for managed hosting than maintain infrastructure.
  • Anyone testing agentic commerce who wants a real, useful service an agent can actually buy.

An honest note on what's managed

"Done for you" means the operator handles the build, hosting, and maintenance with a mix of AI agents and human oversight — not that it runs untouched with zero involvement. Changes that affect your live site or your billing pass through approval gating before they happen. You always have a real workspace, real audits, and a real person-plus-agent operation standing behind the $250/month.
