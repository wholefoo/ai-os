What We Do

AI OS is a Virtual Corporate Headquarters — a complete AI-powered company staffed by 70 specialized agents organized into 11 departments. From a CEO who orchestrates strategy to a Legal department that handles licensing, from an SEO & AEO Agency that audits domains with 5 parallel sub-agents to an AI Web Studio that designs, builds, and hosts static sites, a Knowledge & Records department that governs the company's document library, and a Creative Studio powered by Gemini Omni that generates video, images, and audio.

Unlike single-purpose AI tools, AI OS provides an entire organizational structure. Each agent has a name, a role, a department, a reporting line, and a specific routing tier optimized for their job. The Orchestrator (Atlas, our CEO) routes work to the right agent at the right cost across 6 AI models and 4 routing tiers — strategic decisions go to Claude Opus 5 at maximum reasoning depth, routine lookups go to low-effort scouts, creative generation goes to Gemini Omni, real-time intelligence goes to Grok, and bulk processing goes to the DeepSeek economy tier.
