Business Clones

Your People, Not Just Your Agents

A business clone is modelled after an actual person in your company. An AI agent is a virtual employee that performs a defined function. Both work for you — they are not the same thing, and the difference decides which one you reach for.

🧍

Business Clone — a real person

Modelled after actual company personnel. You build one by being interviewed, not by filling in a form. It carries that person’s voice, their expertise, how they weigh a trade-off, and the limits they set for themselves.

What it knows and what it refuses to say is the product. One clone per person, because a second is a contradictory account of the same human. It drafts in their name and never sends anything — a person reviews every word.

🤖

AI Agent — a defined function

A virtual employee that performs a job. 70 named AI employees across 11 departments — research, SEO auditing, copywriting, data analysis, security review. Each exists because the job exists.

Personality is applied so the output reads like something a person wrote, but the function is the product. Swap the personality and the work still gets done. That is precisely what is not true of a clone.

Clones do not replace agents — a clone directs them. It can commission research or writing from a restricted set of agents, within the limits its person set, and every run passes the same human approval gate as anything else on the platform.

How a Clone Gets Built

The company comes first, so nobody is ever interviewed about the business — every question is about the person.

01

The company profile

The facts and limits every clone on the instance inherits. Type it in, or upload what the business already has written down and accept what AI OS reads out of it, field by field. Limits can only be added this way — never removed or loosened.

02

The founder’s clone

The owner goes first and runs the interview on themselves before asking anyone else to. Because the company is already on file, the questions are about how they talk, what they know, how they decide, and what they will not touch.

03

Your team

Invite colleagues and each builds their own. You see the work their clone produced for the business; you never see their persona — their voice, opinions and personal limits stay theirs.
